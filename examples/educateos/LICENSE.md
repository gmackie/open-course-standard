## Readings

The activity exports carry 150 readings. Each reading's `attribution` field states its source.
At the time of copying, every reading was one of these:

- a **public-domain** text, such as works published before 1930 (Shakespeare, Dickinson, Frost,
  Sandburg), speeches, and U.S. government works such as laws, executive orders, court opinions and
  presidential addresses;
- **written, compiled or paraphrased for EducateOS**, and so covered by the Apache-2.0 license
  above. This includes rounded historical statistics compiled from standard estimates, which are
  facts, and a modern-English adaptation of H. A. R. Gibb's 1929 translation of Ibn Battuta (public
  domain in the United States since 2025).

No reading was found under a license that forbids redistribution. Before adding a new reading,
check its rights and state them in its `attribution`.
